[h1]Climb Ladder / 攀爬梯[/h1]
Version 0.1.2

Workshops on the third floor, housing stacked high, but beavers cannot reach the door?
[b]Climbing Ladder[/b] is a 1×1 path building. The main play is to [b]stack it upward[/b] along a wall or scaffold, linking ground roads to each floor's entrance. Same path tools, same demolition tool — no extra mod UI.

You can lay a few cells flat on one level for a very short gap sometimes; vertical access is what this mod is for.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] Game version 1.1.0.0 or newer
[/list]

Fully quit the game after changing the mod list.

[h2]Unlock & cost[/h2]
Unlock Climbing Ladder in the science panel.
[list]
[*] Science: 100
[*] Build: 1 log per cell
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Paths → Climbing Ladder
[*] From a cell with ground/road, stack upward (three floors / two ladders: door often directly above the top cell; e.g. road at Z=4, stack two cells at the same (x,y) to Z=5 and Z=6, third-floor door at Z=7)
[*] Align the top with the door or one step sideways; connect the bottom to your road network
[*] Press R to rotate when needed
[/olist]

Features:
[list]
[*] 1×1×1 per floor — vertical shafts for tall bases
[*] Can share a cell with vanilla road (no Path slot)
[*] Neighbors usually auto-connect
[*] Box-select demolition removes a full column
[/list]

[h2]vs vanilla stairs[/h2]
[list]
[*] Stairs are bulkier and harder to pack along an outside wall
[*] Ladder segments are real roads — district and doorstep rules apply
[*] Long river bridges: vanilla paths are usually simpler; this mod targets going up
[/list]

[h2]FAQ[/h2]
[b]Upper floor disconnected?[/b]
Road at the base is required; door at the top or directly above the top cell. Construction may delay the warning. On **0.1.2**, fully quit after updating; rebuild top or bottom if needed.

[b]Beavers clip underground, float, or get stuck?[/b]
**0.1.2** fixes many bad nav links; do not run two ladder mods together.

[b]Only for water?[/b]
Short flat spans work, but the mod is built for multi-story vertical links.
